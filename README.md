# Youth Choir Week 2026: the night map of Eket


## Moving around

- **Scroll, swipe up or down, or press the arrow keys:** one gesture flies to the next stop. Going on to a later day, the camera rises above the venue, then travels with a line of light as it draws along the real roads, and comes down into the next venue as the line arrives. Extra scrolling during a flight is ignored.
- **Navbar:** Home, Day 1 to Day 5 and RSVP jump straight to a stop.
- **Drag:** turns the camera around the town (on Home) or around the building (on a day). Drag down to look from higher up. On phones, drag sideways.
- **Zoom:** Ctrl + scroll, a trackpad pinch, a two-finger pinch, or the + and − keys.
- **Idle:** after half a second without input the camera slowly circles on its own (about one degree a second); any input stops it.

The people at each venue are original stylised characters built in `components/scene/People.tsx`, dressed per occasion (`wardrobe`: smart, party or formal).
